# Spike

Spike is the polygonal VR object.

## Default XP
Spike starts with 16 XP.

## XP and shape
For Spike, XP directly determines the number of vertices. A 16-XP Spike therefore has 16 vertices.

## Meta interaction
When a Spike contacts a Meta, it rounds into a circle and becomes a Meta while retaining the Spike's original color. The resulting Meta receives half of the Spike's XP in addition to the Meta XP it is transforming into.

## Glitch interaction
Glitched substance can contact a Spike and transform back into a Spike. XP inheritance for that reverse transformation remains a future tuning point unless explicitly defined by gameplay code.
